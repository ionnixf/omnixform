# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on the work. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of another
person's private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove abusive contributions and may restrict
participation when necessary to keep project spaces safe. Enforcement reports
should use the repository's private maintainer contact once the owner
configures it; do not expose personal or security-sensitive information in a
public issue.

This policy applies in project repositories, reviews, issue discussions, and
other spaces where someone represents the project.
